# AI Skills for GTM and DevRel Teams That Want Output, Not Content Theater

GTM and DevRel don't need more AI content tools. They need repeatable workflows that turn product truth into useful distribution.

Wrong question: *what are the best AI tools for marketing?* That gets you a stack of writing apps, schedulers, and "content engines" that all solve the easy problem — making more words.

The hard problem is turning real product knowledge into the right asset, for the right audience, without losing accuracy, taste, or speed.

A tool produces an asset. A skill makes your agent run the workflow every time. The workflow is the moat.

Every skill below is live on SkillMake. Install or inspect the source from its page.

## Strategy and messaging

- **[Content Strategy](https://skillmake.xyz/marketplace/content-strategy-43765aea)** — decide what to create before you create it. Forces audience research, competitive gaps, and pain points before recommending topics. Stops the random-posting reflex.
- **[Copywriting](https://skillmake.xyz/marketplace/copywriting-eed197dc)** — homepage, landing, pricing, launch copy. Gathers offer and audience context first, then writes. Clearer positioning, not better adjectives.
- **[Grill Me](https://skillmake.xyz/marketplace/mp-grill-me-db92f88e)** — one-question-at-a-time interrogation that exposes the weak parts of a launch narrative, tutorial, or demo script before you ship it. DevRel is teaching; this is how you find what you skipped.

## Ship-quality before you send traffic

- **[Engineering Review](https://skillmake.xyz/marketplace/github-deep-review-9a50e212)** — evidence-first review of demo repos, quickstarts, sample apps, and launch code. Read first, decide stale-or-real, recommend the fix. The user experiences the implementation, not the message.
- **[Design Review](https://skillmake.xyz/marketplace/web-design-guidelines-e5d1ff96)** — UI review against Vercel's interface guidelines: hierarchy, spacing, focus states, forms, motion, mobile. For GTM, design is conversion. For DevRel, design is comprehension. (See also [design-taste-frontend](https://skillmake.xyz/marketplace/design-taste-frontend-0bb1c051) and [redesign-existing-projects](https://skillmake.xyz/marketplace/redesign-existing-projects-96e9890b).)

## Extraction and distribution

- **[Video Transcript to Blog](https://skillmake.xyz/marketplace/video-transcript-to-blog-b42d79da)** — demo, webinar, or interview transcript in, structured post out, with claims, names, and numbers preserved. Your best raw material is already on video; most of it dies there.
- **[Content Repurposing](https://skillmake.xyz/marketplace/content-repurposing-8d75827f)** — one long piece into X threads, LinkedIn posts, Shorts scripts, and newsletter blurbs. Adapts angle by channel; keeps the voice. Not copy-paste across networks.
- **[Release Tweets](https://skillmake.xyz/marketplace/release-tweets-f5768812)** — changelog or tag into launch posts that say what users can now do, not "we shipped X." Pulls from real release artifacts.
- **[Threads + Bluesky Crosspost](https://skillmake.xyz/marketplace/threads-bluesky-crosspost-2e6773f7)** — fan one post out across platforms with consistent voice and correct mechanics (AT Protocol, Threads Graph API). Removes the friction without producing spam.

## Search

- **[SEO Audit](https://skillmake.xyz/marketplace/seo-audit-0a1f7256)** — real audit across crawlability, structure, internal linking, content, and intent. Not "improve your metadata." Fix the site before scaling content on top of it.
- **[Programmatic SEO](https://skillmake.xyz/marketplace/programmatic-seo-0ee714d3)** — pages at scale from templates and data, with unique value per page. Done well: integrations, comparisons, framework guides. Done badly: thin-content spam. This forces the "should this page exist?" check.

## Brand and packaging

- **[Brand System From Reference](https://skillmake.xyz/marketplace/brand-system-from-reference-ae3aa317)** — moodboard or reference site into concrete design tokens (Tailwind v4, CSS vars, Figma). Without one, every asset drifts and every page invents a new visual language.
- **[OG Image Generator](https://skillmake.xyz/marketplace/og-image-generator-793377ef)** — per-post social cards via Satori-style JSX-to-PNG. The card is packaging; generic cards blend into the feed.
- **[Link in Bio](https://skillmake.xyz/marketplace/link-in-bio-a561ea0d)** — owned Linktree alternative with analytics, theming, scheduled links, your domain. Distribution without an owned conversion path is wasted attention.
- **[Gregstyle HyperFrames](https://skillmake.xyz/marketplace/gregstyle-hyperframes-2fe8987f)** — researched 9:16 explainer videos: portrait cards, sparse copy, diagrams, MP4 out. A reusable format so short-form stops being a bespoke edit every time.
